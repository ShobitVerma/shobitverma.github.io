# CAD pose background removal

Tool: built-in image generation, one edit per pose.

Prompt: Remove only the white/light grey gradient background. Preserve the grey mechanism, outlines, gear, handle, tail, feet and joints in the same pose. Make background and gaps transparent, without shadows or recolouring. Centre the complete mechanism with modest padding.

Outputs: cad-pose-1-cutout.png through cad-pose-6-cutout.png. SVG view boxes centre each cutout in the pose viewer.
